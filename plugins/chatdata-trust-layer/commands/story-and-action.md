# Story And Action

Use this after an investigation, WBR prep, or operating brief when the customer needs the answer rewritten for a stakeholder.

Do not add unsupported certainty. Preserve the trust label, caveats, and evidence.

Output:

- headline
- what changed
- why it matters
- what evidence supports it
- what remains uncertain
- owner action or next decision

If the answer path should be reused, run or recommend `/chatdata:sync-context` and `/chatdata:proof`.
